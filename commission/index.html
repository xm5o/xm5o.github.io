How it works

A commission shouldn’t feel confusing.

Send the useful details first, agree on scope and price, then I work from that plan. No mystery middle step.

01

Send the project

Song, engine/version, desired service, difficulty, references, deadline, and anything unusual I should know.

02

Scope + quote

I confirm what I can do, the expected turnaround, revision expectations, and the price before work starts.

03

Build + update

I work on the chart or code and can share progress when it makes sense for the request.

04

Review + delivery

You test the result, request reasonable fixes if needed, and receive the final project files.

Before you message

Send a useful brief and we’ll get to the answer faster.

You do not need a giant document. These six things are enough for me to understand most requests.

01 Song / audio 02 Psych Engine version 03 Service you need 04 Difficulty / style 05 References if you have them 06 Deadline or flexible timing

FAQ

Charting is available, custom coding is handled by quote, and modchart commissions are currently in training rather than being offered as a normal full service.

Most requests fall somewhere between 1 and 14 days. Small charts can be much quicker; larger code work or a busy queue can take longer. I’ll give you a realistic estimate before starting.
