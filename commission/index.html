Frequently Asked Questions

What services do you offer?

I specialize in three main services: Charting, Modcharting (advanced visual effects and note movements), and Custom Coding (Lua scripts and source code modifications for Psych Engine).

How long does it take to complete a project?

Most projects are completed within 1-4 days, depending on complexity. Simple charts and basic modcharts usually take 1-2 days, while complex custom coding or advanced modcharts may take up to 7 days maximum.

What are your payment terms?

I offer flexible payment options - you can choose to pay upfront before I start working on your project, or pay after I complete and deliver your request. The choice is entirely up to you and your preference!

Do you offer revisions?

Yes! I include reasonable revisions in all my projects to ensure you're completely satisfied with the final result. Major changes or complete redesigns may incur additional costs.

How do I get started?

Simply contact me on Discord or use the contact form below with details about your project. Include the song, desired difficulty, any specific requirements, and your timeline. I'll get back to you with a quote and timeline!
